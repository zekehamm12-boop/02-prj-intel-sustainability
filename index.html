INTEL TIMELINE

A Journey of Innovation and Sustainability

1968

1968

Intel Founded

Intel Corporation is formed and begins a new era of technological innovation.

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename the newly formed company NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, laying the foundation for decades of technological innovation.

1971

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel launches the 4004 and changes the future of computing.

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, igniting the microprocessor revolution and propelling the future of computing devices.

1978

1978

8086 Processor

The x86 architecture begins shaping modern computing.

Launch of the 8086 processor establishes the x86 architecture that drives countless PCs and servers in the modern era.

1985

1985

386 Processor

A major leap in performance and multitasking arrives.

Intel introduces the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture, ushering in a new era of performance and multitasking for personal computers.

2006

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel begins aggressively reducing operational emissions.

This year marks Intel's highest annual greenhouse gas emissions for operations. Over subsequent years, Intel invests heavily in chemical abatement, renewable energy, and energy-efficient manufacturing to reverse this trend.

2020

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel sets new goals for responsibility and sustainability.

Intel launches its RISE strategy and 2030 goals, aiming to drive industry-wide progress on climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction.

2022

2022

Net-Zero by 2040

Intel commits to a major long-term emissions goal.

Intel announces its commitment to achieve net-zero greenhouse gas emissions from Scope 1 and Scope 2 operations by 2040.

2023

2023

Renewable Electricity

Intel reaches 99% renewable electricity worldwide.

The company achieves 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide, helping reduce carbon emissions and driving progress toward long-term sustainability goals.

2024

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel brings leaders together to advance sustainable semiconductor manufacturing.

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit, uniting suppliers, government officials, and industry leaders to collaborate on next-generation sustainable semiconductor manufacturing.
